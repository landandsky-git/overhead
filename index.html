Overhead

Point your phone at the night sky to see which satellites are there. Tracks about 16,000 satellites, including Starlink. Gold dots are lit by the sun, and the bigger the dot, the brighter the satellite looks.

Needs location and motion access. Works best outdoors, away from metal and magnets, after waving the phone in a figure-8 to settle the compass.

Show on the sky

Sunlit satellites are drawn bigger and brighter the brighter they should look, on the same scale as the stars. Brightness is estimated from each satellite's type, distance and angle to the sun, so it can be off by about one magnitude.

Compass correction: 0°

Phone compasses point to magnetic north. If the stars on screen sit consistently to one side of the real ones, nudge this until they line up.
